# Project Brief

## Working identity
- Name: Auvraya (provisional; trademark/domain clearance not confirmed)
- Tagline: Ancient wisdom. Everyday balance. (provisional)
- Platform: Android-first
- Languages: English and Hindi

## Product intent
An Ayurveda-inspired wellness and daily-lifestyle companion offering practical seasonal guidance, food/routine education, optional self-reflection, and explainable daily suggestions.

## Intended MVP
- Bilingual onboarding and user preferences
- Optional location by manual city selection; weather only when a suitable provider and terms are confirmed
- Prakriti self-reflection with mixed/uncertain outcomes; do not claim clinical validation without evidence
- Optional current-wellness check-in kept separate from constitutional self-reflection
- Six seasonal guides: Shishira, Vasanta, Grishma, Varsha, Sharad, Hemanta
- Reviewed food and daily-routine catalogue
- Daily plan with clear explanations and source references
- Reminders and habit history
- Saved/offline content where practical
- Profile, privacy controls, and account/data deletion
- CMS for bilingual content, source tracking, reviewer approval, publication, versioning, and rollback

## Not in MVP
- Disease diagnosis or treatment
- An AI doctor or emergency triage substitute
- Prescription or cure claims
- Unvalidated camera, face, tongue, pulse, or other sensor-based diagnosis
- Risky automated herb, supplement, fasting, or cleansing recommendations
- Open supplement marketplace

## Trust and content governance
- Content needs provenance, rights/permission checks, qualified review, review date, and version history.
- Source material supplied by a user is not automatically validated or cleared for commercial reuse.
- Assessment questions and scoring need documented rationale and qualified review before public claims are made.
- AI must not invent citations. Use approved content and show sources where possible.
- Safety rules must be deterministic, reviewed, versioned, and not editable by an unrestricted AI model.

## Key open decisions
- Confirm final brand and legal clearance.
- Confirm backend/CMS technology after repository inspection and cost/privacy review.
- Confirm qualified Ayurveda reviewer and content approval process.
- Confirm weather provider availability, coverage, and terms.
- Define data retention, consent, analytics, and account deletion requirements.
- Decide whether paid features are in scope.
